## Brand and web films

### R25 · First Line

An anime film for a brand identity studio, told as an anime cut is made, in pencil, then ink, then colour: one line on a blank sheet, a room of boiling blue pencil and a flip book of roughs, the mark's four strokes drawing themselves and wiped from pencil to ink to colour, manga panels of a whole identity, and the strokes flying out of a window over Porto at dawn.

Made for: João Queirós · brand identity studio. Stack: Three.js, TSL, Canvas 2D, GSAP, Python (table, score).

[Recipe and poster](../recipes/r25-first-line/) · [Download release](https://github.com/jqaisystems/visions-recipes/releases/tag/r25-first-line-v1)
