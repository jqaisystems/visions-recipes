# R45 · Forage · concept

**Style (season 6, ink feedback, 3 of 3, the finale):** a living ink. Hundreds of thousands of agents steer by the trail they deposit in one WebGL2 feedback buffer, the way slime mould forages. It is the same feedback family as R39 Echo and R42 Blackout, but this time the ink makes decisions: it grows, finds the shortest routes and starves the routes that aren't fed. The simulation runs forward from one anchor at t=0 and replays on a back-seek, so any frame can be rebuilt exactly.

**Made for:** the AI Client Acquisition System (ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/ai-prospecting), João's internal two-part toolset that finds and scores branding leads.

**The idea:** the case study says the bottleneck is "turning 500 cold results into 30 qualified prospects". Slime mould solves that exact problem physically. Spread food across a dish and it first covers everything, then pulls back into a network that feeds only the best sources. So the funnel isn't a chart: it is an organism making up its mind.

**Look:** near-black ground and one bioluminescent yellow, the real colour of the mould, with hard inversions to black ink on yellow on the big hits. Every other colour stays out, so the JQ AI SYSTEMS logo is the only teal when it lands flat at the close. Archivo at 900, JetBrains Mono for labels.

**Beats** (about 34 s):
1. **One spore.** Black. A single point on the first kick, then a few veins feeling their way out. "500 cold results."
2. **The search.** The veins race across the frame and find 500 fictional businesses, each a small dot that flares when reached. The whole frame fills with a living lace, and the camera pulls back as it grows.
3. **Words made of mould.** The agents are fed along letterforms, so the words grow rather than appear: "SEARCH", then "SCORE". The type stays alive and creeping.
4. **The contraction: the wow moment.** Each dot takes a score from 0 to 100, and weak food stops feeding. On the drop the whole network collapses at once: hundreds of veins starve, retract and vanish, leaving 30 thick glowing arteries. The rubric appears as the bands fall away: "below 40 filtered". "500 to 30."
5. **Judgement.** A close-up flies along one artery to one fictional business and its score and written reason, labelled demo data. A hard inversion to black ink on yellow: "SOMETHING SPECIFIC ABOUT THEM."
6. **The email.** An artery reaches an envelope and fills it with a draft that references that business. A person still decides: approve keeps the vein alive, reject cuts it, and the severed end visibly dies. "YOU DECIDE WHAT GOES OUT."
7. **Never twice.** Contacted dots turn repellent, so the living network visibly bends around them as it spreads to a new region. "NO PROSPECT EMAILED TWICE."
8. **Close.** Everything draws back into one point, the opening spore. The logo lands flat, with "AI Client Acquisition System", the URL and "Internal system".

**Sound:** composed in code. A sub drone that thickens as the network grows, a fast wet patter that rises with the reach, one held breath and a huge descending collapse with a sub drop on the contraction, then a tight pulse and clean hits per word, a snip for the rejected vein, and a resolving chord on the logo.

**Honesty rules:**
- Facts come only from the case study: two tools, search by industry and location, 0 to 100 scoring with a written reason, below 40 filtered out (40 to 59 cold, 60 to 79 warm, 80 to 100 strong), a personalised draft, review and approve, no duplicates, stored locally.
- "500 to 30" is the case study's own raw-to-qualified figure. It is shown as what the funnel does. The "three days to under an hour" claim stays out, and no replies, clients or revenue are claimed.
- All businesses, scores and email text are fictional and labelled demo data. No real company appears.
- Generic labels only: no map, email, model or database product names on screen. No third-party logos.
- Internal system: nothing may imply it's for sale as a product or running for clients.
- The film must show a person deciding. Nothing may imply emails send themselves.

**How the film differs from the first idea:** the grown words stay: the letterform clips the light, so the lace visibly lives inside each letter and the words read at once. All other type is crisp, with a halo that keeps veins off it. No product names on screen: the fictional reason says "social page only". The review card offers approve or reject.
