# R17 · Prism · concept

**Style (reusable):** Prism. A glass prism in a black studio, filmed like a perfume commercial: real refraction,
rainbow light across a dark floor. White light is one idea; the prism is your voice; the spectrum is every format it
becomes. Anyone can relabel the colours for their own channels.

**Made for:** AI Content Systems, a service of JQ AI SYSTEMS: a voice specification and an anti-pattern library built
from your own best examples, a pipeline that turns one brief into drafts in every format you publish (each tuned to
its platform, in more than one language), and a review queue where a person approves, rejects or asks for a rewrite
before anything goes out.

**The film:** new for the library, /code film 035. No footage, no images and no third-party names; the pages, brief,
pieces and phrases are made up. The picture and the score are all code, so the whole film ships here.

1. **Your voice.** Lines of your best writing lift off the page as light and become the glass.
2. **Guardrails.** Stock phrases fly at the glass and break.
3. **One brief.** White light enters the prism and splits into a spectrum.
4. **Every format.** Each colour lands as a finished piece: post, thread, newsletter in three languages, blog draft,
   caption, short.
5. **Review.** A person approves each piece; one is rewritten first.
6. **A month.** The pieces fill a month and rise into the word "everywhere".

**Why it travels:** a prism splitting light is the oldest image of one becoming many, and here it is literal. The
phrases breaking on the glass are funny and true, and the review makes the human step visible.

**What the open source leaves out:** only the JQ AI SYSTEMS name and the service's name, line and URL.
