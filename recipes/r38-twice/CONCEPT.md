# R38 · Twice · concept

**Style (season 5, film 3 of 3): time as a material.** It uses the R36 and R37 engine: one WebGL2 fragment shader,
a `sampler2DArray` of frames, and every pixel picks its own moment. The new move: the footage is **a conversation**,
and the film shows the same moment at two times at once. It's also the season's first film on a light ground.

**Made for:** Klatea (ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/klatea), João's private, on-device preparation studio and live
meeting copilot. Its line is "Every big conversation, rehearsed before it happens."

**The screens:** the published film uses Klatea's own screens of its fictional demo scenario, which are not in this
repo. Here, `bake/demo.py` draws a fictional app running a fictional call with "Example Co", with every outlined
element where the table expects it, and `bake/prep.py` turns the screens into slow shots.

**The idea:** the hard moment of a call should happen twice, first in rehearsal, then for real. A slit-scan can
show that literally: one window is the rehearsal, the other the live call, and the same objection arrives in both
on the same beat. The film treats the meeting as a length of time: prepared, rehearsed, lived, and folded into a
debrief that becomes the next prep.

1. **Prepare.** Paper ground, a hairline slit in ink. The prep form plays and the brief writes itself out of the
   slit as a time smear. "Every big conversation,"
2. **Rehearse.** A scan brings in the role-play; the client's question is cut out of time and held as a sage
   outline while the rest of the screen moves on, with the feedback under it. "rehearsed out loud,"
3. **Twice.** The window splits into rehearsal and live, the same objection lands in both, then the slit sweeps it
   all live, the LIVE dot comes on and the help from the prep lands. "before it happens."
4. **Both sides.** Each side of the transcript is displaced in time by its own speaker; talk time settles and a
   line is translated on the machine. "Audio stays on your computer."
5. **Debrief.** The whole call slit-scanned into one image, squeezed to a strip labelled by section and unfolded
   into the debrief, its lines outlined as they are read. "Then an honest debrief,"
6. **The next one.** A tunnel built from the call's screens comes out on a new prep. "and the prep for the next one."
7. **Title.** The name in letters that each show a different moment of the call, then the mark, flat, with the
   line, the URL and "Free Windows beta".

**Look:** paper #F4F1EA, ink #114353 for the slit and headlines, sage #6B8A67 for the held moments, coral #D64550
only on the LIVE dot and the closing line; Fraunces with a sage italic accent word, IBM Plex Mono labels and a
rotated margin rail; no glow, gradients or shadows. The time effects stay crisp: hard slits and clean smears.

**Sound:** a score composed in code at 88 bpm: a felt piano and a soft pad, a page-turn rustle, a two-note motif
for the objection played alone in rehearsal and then against its own echo when the frame splits, a swell as it goes
live, two filtered murmurs for the two sides (never speech), a click for each debrief line and a resolving chord.

**Honesty:** the published film's figures (8/10, 44% talk time, the two-week pilot) are the demo scenario's own
screens, not results, and its client is the app's demo, not a real company. No real meeting appears.
