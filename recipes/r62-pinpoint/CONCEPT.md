# R62 · Pinpoint · concept

**Made for:** the AI Lead Generation & Outreach service (ai.joaoqueiros.com/services/lead-generation). The film sells the service, so it ends with the service link.

**Style:** kinetic motion graphics, the showreel kind: pure vector, hard colour flips between scenes (ink, bone, teal, coral), big type that slams in letter by letter on the beat, and real motion blur (each frame is eight sub-frames across a half-frame shutter, averaged), the look most code-made films lack.

**The idea:** one coral dot is the hero and is never cut away from. Every scene hands it to the next, so the film is one continuous object moving through the pipeline.

**Beats:**
1. **Open:** a reticle waits; the dot drops into it, bounces, gathers itself and bursts into 2,304 prospects that streak out to their places.
2. **01 Find:** "FIND" slams in. A lens sweeps the field, magnifying as it goes; the good fits light teal and a counter climbs. It locks on the coral dot ("PROSPECT 0412 · FIT 87") and the camera dives into it. "PROSPECTS THAT MATCH YOUR IDEAL CUSTOMER".
3. **02 Score:** inside the dot is a bone page. A gauge draws itself tick by tick; the dot becomes its needle and swings to 87. The gauge drops apart, the needle becomes a bar, seven more bars rise and sort themselves, warm ones first. "EVERY PROSPECT SCORED FOR FIT, 0 TO 100" / "WARM LEADS FIRST, NOT A RANDOM PILE".
4. **03 Enrich:** a teal wipe opens out of the dot; the top bar becomes a record card, and each detail slams in from the edges and is ticked. "EACH RECORD ENRICHED WITH PUBLIC EVIDENCE".
5. **04 Draft:** the card flips; its back is a message that writes itself with the dot as the cursor, the words taken from the record underlined in coral, then stamped "DRAFT · FOR REVIEW". "A PERSONALISED FIRST MESSAGE" / "READY FOR A PERSON TO REVIEW". It folds into a paper plane and flies off.
6. **05 Your call:** the plane flies at a giant SEND button and freezes short of it; the music stops dead. "NOTHING SENDS AUTOMATICALLY." types itself into the silence. The dot leaves the plane and rests beside the button, never pressing it. "YOU KEEP THE SEND BUTTON." / "WAITING FOR YOUR REVIEW".
7. **Close:** the frame folds into the dot, the dot pops, and the logo, "AI Lead Generation & Outreach", the URL and "A SERVICE · THIS FILM IS CODE".

**Technique:** one Canvas 2D stage, every frame a pure function of the beat (`index.html`); one table (`bake/table.py`) holds every cue, including the beat each letter lands and each key is typed, so `score/compose.py` hits them exactly. The score is synthesised in code (kick, clap, hats, bass, arp and pads in A minor, plus every slam, tick, key click and whoosh), and cuts to a single held note at the freeze.

**Honesty rules:**
- **Facts only from the service page:** prospects that match the ideal customer; each enriched with public evidence; scored for fit from 0 to 100; warm leads first, not a random pile; a personalised first message drafted for a person to review; nothing sends automatically, you keep the send button.
- **Demo content:** the prospects, "Halden Studio", "Ana" and the message are made up. On-screen label: "AI LEAD GENERATION & OUTREACH · DEMO PIPELINE".
- **Nothing named or claimed:** no source, tool, model or platform names, no results, no prices, no guarantees, no case study or live example.
