# R50 · Full Stop · concept

**Style (stand-alone):** pure motion graphics. Every frame is a pure function of the beat, drawn in Canvas 2D with per-letter type, exposed over 12 to 24 sub-frames for real motion blur, then printed by one WebGL2 pass that can turn the frame into halftone, dither, ASCII, a line screen, pixels, riso or neon. No simulation, no footage, no 3D engine.

**Made for:** the AI Automation Systems service (ai.joaoqueiros.com/services/ai-automation).

**The idea:** the hero is a full stop. One red dot ends every sentence in the film, and it won't stay put. A full stop that keeps going is a task that keeps running. It stops once, on "YOURS."

**Why it's a wow:** one character carries the whole reel, so every style change feels like the same story. The craft is on show as classic animation principles (squash, stretch, anticipation, overlap) and as twenty cuts in sixteen graphic styles in ten beats that get faster and faster.

**Beats** (75 at 112.5 bpm, 40 s):
1. **The same task.** The dot falls onto empty paper, bounces, then dashes right three times and drags "THE SAME TASK" out behind it, ending as its full stop.
2. **Again.** It jumps off, the sentence falls away, and it lands to raise "AGAIN." Then the same move for "AND AGAIN."
3. **Doubling.** One tile becomes 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, each running the same job. The camera falls into one dot until the screen is red.
4. **Plain English.** Karaoke: the dot lands on each word of "IF YOU CAN DESCRIBE IT" and the word gets up to meet it.
5. **The map.** The dot draws the workflow, node by node, and the camera rolls at each corner. It pulls back to the whole map, and the dot flies off to end "MAPPED BEFORE ANY CODE."
6. **Code.** The dot is the dot in `task.run()`. Then "CUSTOM PYTHON."
7. **Not a template.** The same words and the same bounce in twenty cuts across sixteen styles: Swiss, halftone, extrusion, dither, moiré, ASCII, slices, neon, width stack, riso, Bauhaus, pixel, warped checker, echo outlines, gradient line screen, blueprint. One a beat, then two, then four.
8. **Nothing.** Black, one dot, two ticks.
9. **On your schedule.** A clock fills hour by hour, then you fall through nested clock faces as the day goes from morning to night, with the dot orbiting ever faster.
10. **Handed over.** "EVERY FILE." "EVERY PROMPT." "EVERY CONFIG." Sheets stack, prompts slide, switches flip.
11. **Yours.** Five letters, five slams. The dot comes in from far away, lands, bounces smaller and smaller, and stops. So does the music. Real silence, then dark spreads from the dot.
12. **Card.** The dot travels to end "AI Automation Systems." Logo flat and untouched, the URL, "A SERVICE · THIS FILM IS CODE".

**Sound:** composed in code from the same timing table: a rubber ball for every landing (bigger landings go lower), a tight funk kit and slap bass in A minor, marimba for the nodes, and a different instrument for each style in the roll. It should sound nothing like Ferro or Hands Off.

**Honesty rules:** as on the Hands Off card. Facts come only from the service page, the workflow, prompts, code and clock times are demo and labelled, no results, no third-party names, and Python appears only as the language the page names. The clock carries "SCHEDULED RUNS, IF YOU WANT THEM", because the page says they're optional.

**Generated, not in the repo:** `assets/bgm/score.wav`, renders.
