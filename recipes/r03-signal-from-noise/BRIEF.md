## Intent

R03 of the Recipes library on visions.joaoqueiros.com (`/recipes`, also `/code`). A film for AI News Curator
(ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/ai-news-curator), JQ AI SYSTEMS' internal research briefing workflow. About 6,000
fragments drift as noise, line up along a field like iron filings, gather around the six real themes, and the
strongest six become one reviewed daily brief before the rest settles into an archive by day. Concept approved
by João 2026-10-22 (`CONCEPT.md`).
