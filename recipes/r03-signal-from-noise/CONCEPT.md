# R03 · Signal from Noise · concept card (approved by João 2026-10-22: AI News Curator, case-study headline, six themes)

**Style (reusable):** Signal from Noise. Thousands of small fragments behave like iron filings. A field switches
on, and they turn, line up and gather around the poles that matter. Anyone can swap the fragments, the poles and
what they finally form.

**Subject:** AI News Curator (`/systems/ai-news-curator`, Live · Internal, JQ Internal, 2026). Case study headline:
"Scattered signals, daily briefings." Its real workflow: import, normalize, classify, brief, review, decide,
archive. Its real themes: AI tooling, coding workflows, design systems, automation patterns, public GitHub work,
client research. Its real review outcomes: public content, private research, client context, archived note.

**Why not SEO Engine:** its case study already has a film. The News Curator has none, and its headline is
literally this style.

**Story in one line:** a storm of scattered signals (releases, newsletters, docs updates, saved links) drifts as
noise. A field switches on and every fragment turns to face it, gathers into six topics, and the strongest few
are pulled into one daily brief. A human gate sorts it, and the rest settles into an archive by day.

**Spec:** about 26 s, 16:9 1920×1080, 30 fps. Score composed in code (a low magnetic hum, granular metallic
ticks as the filings turn, one soft thunk per pole, a calm pad for the brief). Same MIT source routine on GitHub.

**Look:** the ai site's dark palette (#111110, #F5F5F0, teal #00A89D). The filings are iron grey (#6E6E68)
while they are noise and turn bone, then teal, as they line up. Geist and Geist Mono, Instrument Serif for the
final line. Technique: Canvas 2D only, so it looks and builds nothing like R01 (Three.js) or R02 (CSS 3D).

**Safety:** no real saved links, no invented headlines and no private sources. Fragments carry only source
types ("github · release", "newsletter", "docs update", "saved link", "community note") and the six real theme
names, matching the case study's public-safe rule.

## Why it fits
The product's job (turn scattered reading into a few reviewed signals) is the physics of the film. Every frame
is computed from time (seeded noise plus a field), so the recipe is easy to remix: change the labels and poles,
and the same field sorts anything.
