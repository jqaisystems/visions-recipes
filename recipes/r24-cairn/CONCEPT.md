# R24 · Cairn · concept

**Style (reusable):** cel-shaded anime with painted background art. This is the second of three films in this style.
It builds on R23 with:
- a painted multiplane mountain panorama;
- a cel sea of cloud;
- a banded sky placed against the true horizon;
- time-lapse light with cast shadows;
- manga lettering.

**Made for:** Cairn (ai.joaoqueiros.com/cairn), João Queirós' done-for-you SEO service. The mark is six stones
stacked into a C. A cairn marks the way on a mountain trail, and each checkpoint adds a stone. Page line: "Search,
checked every four weeks."

1. **Forty numbers.** At night on the summit, dashboard numbers hail down and shatter. A gust blows them away, and
   three cards stay: Indexed, Buyer searches, Enquiries.
2. **The first stone.** The camera tilts up into a dawn-lit cloud bank, and the first stone falls and lands. A trail
   sign reads START · #18 · Page 2.
3. **Every four weeks.** A time-lapse. A stone lands at week 4 (#9) and at week 8 (#3). The positions are labelled
   as an illustration.
4. **The checkpoint.** Four decisions from a real checkpoint (CP-03, 13 Oct 2026) slam in as lettering: GO, NO-GO,
   VOID, CLOSED.
5. **The summit.** The last stones land and the sun breaks through. The camera cranes up over the sea of cloud.
6. **Title.** The flat lockup.

**Brand rules kept in motion:**
- The camera moves, never the mark.
- No stone is recoloured.
- Clay red is used only for NO-GO.
- The end lockup is flat.

**What the open source leaves out:** nothing. The logo and the names are not licensed.
