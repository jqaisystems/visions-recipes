## Intent

R01 of the Recipes library on visions.joaoqueiros.com (`/recipes`, also `/code`). A film for the Prompt
Engineering service (service 12 on ai.joaoqueiros.com). A generic AI paragraph has its clichés caught by
the anti-pattern library; they dissolve into token particles, rain through the chain (draft, critique,
rewrite) and recompile as one line in the brand's voice. It must make people say "wow", and its prompt,
breakdown and MIT source zip get shared. Concept approved by João 2026-10-21 (`CONCEPT.md`).

- Three.js WebGPURenderer + TSL particles on its WebGL2 backend (spike passed 2026-10-21: renders on GPU,
  two renders matched at PSNR 57 dB).
