# R25 · First Line · concept

**Style (reusable):** cel-shaded anime. This is the third of three films in this style, the finale. It builds on R23
and R24 with:
- the anime production pipeline as the look: one 3D world rendered as a pencil pass, an ink pass and a colour pass,
  wiped from one to the next;
- a flip book of roughs;
- ribbon geometry that draws itself along the mark's own paths;
- a painted Porto, from blue hour to dawn.

**Made for:** joaoqueiros.com, João Queirós' brand identity studio. The mark is a JQ monogram drawn as four parallel
strokes in a teal to cyan gradient (#00A79D to #0095DA) on black #0D1114. Site line: "Brand identity design for
businesses ready to grow." From Porto to clients worldwide; 1,200+ brands, 40+ countries, since 2014 (the homepage's
own figures).

1. **The brief.** Night, a desk by a window over the Douro. A blank sheet; a pencil draws one line. Impact frame.
2. **Roughs.** The world turns to blue pencil. Rough marks pile up and a flip book of roughs riffles, all abstract.
3. **Ink and colour.** The four strokes draw themselves, lift off the paper and are wiped from pencil to ink to
   colour. The mark locks front-on.
4. **The system.** Manga panels slam in on the beat: a generic identity on a card, a box, a sign, a bag, a label and
   a phone, stamped LOGO, COLOUR, TYPE, PACKAGING, LABELS, SOCIAL.
5. **Worldwide.** Out of the window over Porto at dawn, the strokes fly into the sun as trails and the figures land.
6. **Title.** The official logo, flat, with the line and joaoqueiros.com.

**Brand rules kept in motion:**
- The logo is never stretched, redrawn, cropped or recoloured: the ribbons follow the official paths and gradients,
  and the end lockup is the official file, flat.
- The camera moves around the mark; the mark does not spin.

**Honesty:** the figures are the homepage's own. The roughs and the applications are generic; no client work appears.

**What the open source leaves out:** nothing. The logo and the name are not licensed.
