# R20 · Cartographer · concept

**Style (reusable):** Cartographer. A navigator's chart table in a dark study, filmed in macro like a watch commercial:
a relief map of the business rises out of the table as a field of thousands of small columns, brass dividers and a
compass rose, lamp light, fog over the parts nobody has mapped yet. The audit lifts the fog, the opportunities are
surveyed as pins whose height is their score, and the route is ruled in phases. Anyone can relabel the regions and
the pins for their own plan.

**Made for:** AI Consulting & Roadmapping (ai.joaoqueiros.com/services/consulting), a service of JQ AI SYSTEMS: an
audit of where the hours actually go, a sweep of what AI can realistically do now, every opportunity scored by
payoff, cost and risk, a ranked list with the reasoning, a phased plan of what to build first, second and third, and a
90-minute debrief. No code, just direction, and vendor-neutral.

**The film:** new for the library, /code film 038. No footage, no images and no third-party names; the business, the
regions and the opportunities are made up. The picture and the score are all code, so the whole film ships here.

1. **Not sure where to start.** Darkness, then a lamp comes up over a chart table: a flat map of one made-up business,
   its regions named ("Sales", "Quotes", "Support", "Finance", "Content", "Hiring"), all under fog. A teal HUD:
   "where does AI fit?".
2. **The audit.** A brass survey ring sweeps the table. Where it passes, the fog lifts and the land rises: the columns
   climb into a relief whose height is where the hours actually go. Peaks form over the work that eats the week.
3. **The opportunities.** Pins drop onto the relief, one per opportunity (about a dozen), each a slim brass needle with
   a teal head; three small rings around each settle to its payoff, cost and risk. A few pins are knocked flat: the
   honest "not worth building".
4. **Ranked.** The camera rises overhead. The standing pins lift off the map and line up along the table's edge in
   rank order, tallest first, each with its one-line reason on a paper tag.
5. **The route.** Brass dividers walk across the map from the top-ranked pin, ruling a teal route in three legs, each
   leg a phase ("First: 0 to 3 months", "Second: 3 to 6", "Third: 6 to 12"); quick wins light up along the first leg,
   the bigger plays wait at the far end. A compass needle swings and settles on the first leg.
6. **The roadmap.** The map's paper rolls up into a slim document tied with teal bands and a tag, "Roadmap · debrief
   booked", and the route's line lifts off the table and draws the word "mapped".

**Why it travels:** everyone knows a map with "here be dragons" at the edges. It turns a strategy engagement into one
image: fog over the business, then the land, the pins, and a route you can follow. The pins knocked flat show the
honest part of the service: some AI ideas are not worth building.

**Rules for this film:** no tool, vendor or model names anywhere. No promised savings, hours or ROI: the relief and
the pins are relative heights with no numbers, and the phases are months, not results. The business, its regions and its
opportunities are made up and generic.

**What the open source leaves out:** only the JQ AI SYSTEMS name and the service's name, line and URL.
