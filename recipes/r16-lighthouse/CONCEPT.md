# R16 · Lighthouse · concept

**Style (reusable):** Lighthouse. A lighthouse on a dark sea at night, filmed like a luxury watch commercial: a brass
lantern room with a turning glass lens, a slow sweeping beam, boat lights scattered far out on the water. The beam is
the search; the boats are prospects; the harbour is the pipeline. Anyone can relabel the boats for their own
customers.

**Made for:** AI Lead Generation & Outreach, a service of JQ AI SYSTEMS: a system that finds prospects that match your
ideal customer, enriches each one with public evidence, scores fit from 0 to 100 so the hot ones come first, and
drafts a personalised first message for a person to review. Nothing sends automatically.

**The film:** new for the library, /code film 034. No footage, no images and no third-party names; the prospects,
cards and message are made up. The picture and the score are all code, so the whole film ships here.

1. **Night.** A macro through the lantern glass: the lamp flickers on, the lens starts to turn, a foghorn.
2. **Source.** The beam sweeps the sea and every boat it passes lights up.
3. **Enrich.** The beam stops on three boats; a card of public evidence rises beside each.
4. **Score.** A top view: every boat gets a score from 0 to 100; the cold ones fade, the hot ones turn for shore.
5. **Draft.** A first message writes itself for the best lead, and a person pulls the brass SEND lever.
6. **Harbour.** The hot leads moor at the quay and their lights rise into the word "found", mirrored in the sea.

**Why it travels:** a lighthouse beam on a dark sea is instantly beautiful and instantly understood: searching,
finding, guiding in. Scores floating over the water are a new image, and the lever makes the human step physical.

**What the open source leaves out:** only the JQ AI SYSTEMS name and the service's name, line and URL.
