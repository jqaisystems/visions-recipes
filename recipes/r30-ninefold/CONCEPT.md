# R30 · Ninefold · concept

Season 3, film 2 of 3. Made for Grid Studio beside Seamless (R29, `R29-seamless-CONCEPT.md`), first to compare and
then released as its own recipe.

**Style (season 3, film 2 of 3): raymarched glass field.** It still uses one fragment shader with no meshes, but its showpieces differ
from Seamless:
- **An infinite field from one cell:** domain repetition, with the nearest four cells evaluated per step.
- **Refractive glass:** three rays, one per colour, read the photo at each tile's base, so the picture splits into
  colour at every bevel.
- **A wave on every beat**, with each tile tilting to its slope.
- **A polar fold for the kaleidoscope.**
- **A per-cell shape blend for the tools:** box, sphere, crossed bars, lines, squircle and QR bits.
- **Speed-ramped camera moves:** a pull-back, a dolly zoom down a column, and hard cuts with flash frames.
- **Kinetic type slammed on the beat** in heavy Archivo, set against Instrument Serif.

**Made for:** Grid Studio (ai.joaoqueiros.com/gridstudio), twelve free Instagram tools that run in the browser.

**The idea:** one image, ninefold. Where Seamless pours and melts, Ninefold cuts and multiplies: one tile becomes nine,
nine become an endless field, and the field folds back into one tile and then into the mark.

1. **Open (0 to 2.8 s).** One glass tile spins out of black with the coastline photo split into colour through it.
   "One image." It bursts into nine.
2. **Field (2.8 to 8 s).** The camera speed-ramps back over an endless field of glass tiles, and a wave rolls out on
   every beat. "Nine posts." "Zero seams."
3. **Feed (8 to 14 s).** A dolly zoom runs down a profile column toward an empty 3×3 slot, then a hard cut to straight
   above. Nine tiles drop in posting order, post 1 bottom-right in terracotta, and the seams flash. "Posted in order."
   "Seamless on your profile."
4. **Tools (14 to 22 s).** Ten hard cuts, one every beat and a half, each a new shape of the same field: Grid Maker,
   Carousel, Covers, Palette, Resize, QR Code, Icon Maker, Captions, Hashtags and Bio. Then the whole field returns:
   "Twelve free tools."
5. **In your browser (22 to 27 s).** A kaleidoscope folds the field into a mandala, and it collapses into one tile
   inside a browser window. UPLOAD, ACCOUNT and WATERMARK each fly at the glass and shatter on it. "No upload. No
   account. No watermark."
6. **Title (27 to 32 s).** The tile becomes nine, which lock into the mark at the official file's size. Then the flat
   official mark, "Grid Studio", "Twelve free Instagram tools in your browser" and ai.joaoqueiros.com/gridstudio.

**Score:** 128 bpm, D minor opening to F major.
- A sub hit and breaking glass on the burst.
- A glass ting on every wave.
- A riser down the dolly zoom, and a block plus a pluck for every post.
- A sub and a climbing ting on every tool cut.
- A whoosh, a sub and glass for each shattered word.
- An F major chord and a bell on the mark.

It is mastered to Seamless's level, so the two films compare fairly.

**Honesty and brand** (same rules as Seamless):
- The photo is the app's demo image (Stefan Kunze, Unsplash License).
- Only tool names the app lists. "Twelve" is the product's own number, with no metrics.
- The mark is the official file, placed flat.
