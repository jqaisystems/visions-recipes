# The picture

The film reads one image from this folder: `demo-banner.jpg`. It is not included, because the photo in the published
film belongs to its photographer (Stefan Kunze, Unsplash License) and is not part of this repo's licence.

Add any image of your own here as `demo-banner.jpg` before you preview or render. A portrait image at 3:4 works best
(the film used 900×1200), because the grid cuts it into a 3×3 of 3:4 tiles. Without it, the film does not start.
