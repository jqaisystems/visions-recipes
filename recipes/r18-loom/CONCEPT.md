# R18 · Loom · concept

**Style (reusable):** Loom. A Jacquard loom in a black studio, filmed in macro like a watch commercial: brass, oiled
wood, taut threads of light. Every source is a thread; what you know is the punched card that tells the loom which
threads to lift; the woven cloth is the briefing. A nod to the punched card, the first program ever written for a
machine. Anyone can relabel the threads for their own sources.

**Made for:** AI Research & Briefing Systems, a service of JQ AI SYSTEMS: a system that monitors the sources that
matter (news, industry sites, competitor pages, feeds), reads a document of what you already know before it reads the
news, filters the noise, summarises what changed with the why it matters, and delivers a briefing in your format on a
schedule, such as every Monday at 09:00.

**The film:** new for the library, /code film 036. No footage, no images and no third-party names; the sources,
headlines, rules and stories are made up. The picture and the score are all code, so the whole film ships here.

1. **Your sources.** Hundreds of threads of light stream in from the dark onto the loom, headlines flickering past.
2. **What you know.** A brass card is punched with the rules, one hole at a time, and read before the news.
3. **Only what matters.** The loom runs; nine threads are lifted, and the other 203 go slack and fall away.
4. **The briefing.** The cloth is woven, and its type comes through: three stories, each with the why, each over its
   own source threads.
5. **On schedule.** A clock strikes Monday 09:00; the cloth is cut, folded and carried to a table before the meeting.
6. **Every Monday.** The briefings stack up, and the threads gather into the word "briefed".

**Why it travels:** a loom is the oldest machine that reads instructions, and here the image is literal: the threads
are the sources, the card is what you know, the cloth is the briefing. The threads falling away make "filters the
noise" visible in one shot, and the clock makes the schedule tangible.

**What the open source leaves out:** only the JQ AI SYSTEMS name and the service's name, line and URL.
