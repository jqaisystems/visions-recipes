# R11 · Kinetic Index · concept

**Style (reusable):** Kinetic Index. A prompt types out, then rows of titles pour from its line at four sizes,
each row at its own speed and direction. A search box types queries that land on the beat: the matching titles
light up with the match underlined while the rest fade back. Then the river rushes and tilts into a floor under
a headline that drops in word by word. Any list of words becomes the film: posts, products, a menu, a changelog.

**The film:** the intro for the blog on Imagined Worlds, /code film 018. It has no footage, no images and no
logo, and the score is composed in code, so the whole film ships here.

**What changed for this repo:** the film on the site shows my real post titles, which name other companies'
products. Here the titles are a made-up demo list (`titles.txt`), and the three search words and the score's key
ticks change to match it. The prompt and the headline stay as the example; swap them for your own.
