# R53 · Channels · concept

**Style (season 8, sand on a lightbox, film 3 of 3, the finale):** the same lightbox, and the two halves of the season finally working together. R51 was the hand drawing in sand, and R52 was the sand flowing by itself. Here the fingertip carves channels into a sheet of sand, and loose grains run down them like water down a riverbed, so what the hand draws decides where the sand goes. It's a new kind of motion for the season: not pictures morphing, but a steady stream that splits, joins, backs up and runs again.

**Made for:** the AI Workflow Integration service (ai.joaoqueiros.com/services/workflow-integration). This film sells the service, so it ends with the service link.

**The idea:** your tools stay where they are, and the connections between them get smart. On the table, the tools are islands of sand and the work between them is grains that get stuck. The service doesn't replace an island. It cuts the channels between them and puts one decision point in the middle.

**Why it's a wow:** the flow. Thousands of terracotta grains pouring along channels the viewer has just watched being cut, splitting at a fork, filling a basin and spilling into the next channel exactly on the beat. Then a shadow channel runs beside the old one before the old one is wiped away.

**Beats** (about 36 s):
1. **The islands.** Black, a click, amber. Five islands of dark sand, each a plain shape: a page, a grid, a speech bubble, an envelope, a card. Terracotta grains pile up between them with nowhere to go. "YOUR TOOLS."
2. **The map.** The fingertip draws a one-page map in thin lines: the islands, the arrows between them, one circle in the middle. It taps the corner. "MAPPED ON ONE PAGE." "YOU APPROVE IT FIRST."
3. **The channels.** The finger cuts real channels along the drawn arrows, deep enough that the light comes through. The stuck grains start to move. "NOTHING REPLACED. JUST CONNECTED."
4. **The decision layer (the showpiece).** Every stream runs into the circle in the middle and leaves split: some to the grid, some to the card, some to the envelope with a short trail of a reply drafted beside it. A pulse of grains takes the fork to a small figure of a person. "ROUTED. SUMMARISED. TO THE RIGHT PERSON."
5. **When it fails.** One channel is blocked by a fallen ridge. The grains back up, and instead of vanishing they light a small mark beside it, and the music stops on one bell. "EVERY RUN LOGGED." "FAILURES ALERT. NOTHING DROPS SILENTLY."
6. **Shadow run.** A second channel is cut beside the old route, and both run side by side, the grains arriving at the same time. Then the finger sweeps the old one away. "SHADOW RUN, THEN CUTOVER."
7. **Yours.** The table settles into one connected landscape, with the streams still running, and a folder is laid in sand at the edge: "THE CODE. THE KEYS. THE DOCS. YOURS." The light clicks off. The logo is placed flat, with "AI Workflow Integration", the URL and "A SERVICE · THIS FILM IS CODE".

**Sound:** composed in code, the same family as R51 and R52, so the season sounds like one set: plucked strings, hand drum and filtered-noise sand. Its new voice is the trickle: each stream is grain noise that follows the flow, so you can hear a channel open, fill and split. The fork splits the melody into two lines, the failure is one bell over silence, and the shadow run plays the same phrase twice in unison before one copy is cut.

**Look:** near-black sand on the amber table, with the terracotta grains for the work moving through it, as in R52. Channels are cut deep enough to glow, so the network reads as lines of light. Islands are shapes only. A small flat label stays on screen for honesty.

**Honesty rules:**
- Facts come only from the service page: an AI decision layer added to the tools a team already uses, without replacing them; a stack audit and workflow map; a one-page design the client approves before building; routing, categorising, summarising and classifying; routing inbound requests to the right person with a drafted reply; event-driven or scheduled triggers; every run logged and failures alerted rather than dropped silently; a shadow run alongside the old workflow before cutover; source code, configuration, documentation and a walkthrough handed over; credentials owned by the client.
- A label stays on screen: "AI WORKFLOW INTEGRATION · DEMO WORKFLOW". The workflow, the requests and the reply are fictional. The end card says "A SERVICE · THIS FILM IS CODE".
- No third-party names or logos: the tools are shapes only (a page, a grid, a bubble, an envelope, a card), and no platform, model provider or connector is named or hinted by a logo.
- No results: no time saved, no prices, no build times, no warranty length and no client work.
- "Nothing drops silently" is said only about failures alerting, exactly as the page puts it, never as a promise that nothing ever fails.

**Generated, not in the repo:** `assets/table.js`, `assets/bgm/score.wav`, renders.

**How the film differs from the first idea:** three kinds of grain. 300,000 dark grains are a sheet of sand with the five islands raised in it, and every cut the fingertip makes pushes them aside into ridges, worked out from the time alone. 36,000 dark grains are the captions, flowing from one to the next by sliced optimal transport as in R52. 100,000 terracotta grains are the work: each one has a route, a time it lands in a pile, a time it leaves, a lane and a speed, and the channels' ridges are what make the streams read as sand and not as dots on rails. The map's thin lines widen into the channels from the sources outwards. Arriving work rolls onto its tool and settles in as a terracotta speckle, so the tools fill up as the film goes on. The folder at the edge in beat 7 is left out: the captions carry "THE CODE. THE KEYS. THE DOCS. YOURS." on two lines. The tick that approves the map is cut at the bottom of the table. The film is 36.25 s at 96 bpm.
