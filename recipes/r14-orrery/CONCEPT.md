# R14 · Orrery · concept

**Style (reusable):** Orrery. A system of specialists shown as a brass-and-glass clockwork model of the heavens. The
center is the orchestrator; each specialist is a glass planet on its own gimballed ring, with a moon for its memory and
small satellites for its skills. Work travels between them as light, and the payoff is a conjunction: every ring snaps
into one plane and the result fires out as a beam. Anyone can swap the planets for their own team, product modules or
departments.

**Made for:** AI Agent Architecture, a service of JQ AI SYSTEMS: multi-agent systems where each agent has a role,
memory and skills, with clear handoffs, an orchestrator and an escalation path to a person.

**The film:** new for the library, /code film 032. No footage, no images and no client material; the picture and the
score are all code, so the whole film ships here.
