# R61 · Stacked

**Style:** a shadow sculpture built on camera: a heap of small blocks rises into a lattice row by row, carved so three level lamps each throw a word onto their own wall, and all three words write themselves from the bottom up at once. The lattice rebuilds twice.
Every frame is worked out from the time alone. No footage: every word is a real shadow.
**Made for:** AI Stack Setup & Enablement, a service: for teams whose AI use is scattered across personal accounts and pasted prompts, it chooses and installs the right tools, configures them with custom skills, prompt libraries and a usage playbook, and runs a hands-on onboarding with real tasks. Everything runs under the team's own accounts and keys.
**Length:** 39.2 s · 1920×1080 · 30 fps, with a 9:16 cut re-laid for portrait.
**Stack:** HyperFrames · WebGL2 · Canvas 2D · GSAP · Python (carving, table and score) · FFmpeg.
**Cost:** €0. Everything here is free and open source.

## The prompt

```
Make a 39-second 1920x1080 film in HyperFrames for AI Stack Setup & Enablement, a service that sets up AI for a team whose use is scattered across personal accounts and pasted prompts: it chooses and installs the right tools for the work, configures each with custom skills, prompt libraries and a usage playbook, and runs a hands-on onboarding where the team works through real tasks, not a slideshow. Everything runs under the team's own accounts and keys, and they keep it all. Make it a shadow sculpture built on camera: a heap of small blocks that rises into a lattice, carved so three level lamps each throw a different word.
Carve it in Python first, three times. Draw each word as a mask at the same letter proportions: CODE, VIDEO and IMAGE; SKILLS, PROMPTS and PLAYBOOK; YOUR, OWN and STACK. Keep the lamps level, at 0°, 45° and 90°, so every shadow shares the height axis and each row of the lattice casts the same row of all three words. The solid is where all three masks, pushed along the lamps' lines, say yes. Thin it one block at a time, in a seeded order, for as long as every shadow stays exactly the same. Match each lattice's blocks to the next one's, in the same row where they can be, so a rebuild slides every block to its next place.
Draw every block instanced in WebGL2 with softly bevelled edges, able to lie at any angle, with one shadow map per lamp, in a dark room with three plaster walls, each square on to one lamp. Give every block of the first lattice a place in a loose heap on the floor, and build the lattice one row at a time from the floor up, a dotted sixteenth apart, taking the top of the heap first: so all three words write themselves from the bottom up at once. Work out every block from the time alone. One table holds the rows, the rebuilds, the team, the camera and the captions, and drives both the film and the score, at 104 bpm. An "AI STACK SETUP & ENABLEMENT · DEMO STACK" label stays on screen. The score is composed in code, dry and wooden: a hum for each lamp, a rattle for the heap, and a tuned knock as each row locks, a step higher each time, so the build is a rising scale.
Scattered: three lamps click on over a heap of blocks on the floor. The walls show only low, lumpy noise. "SCATTERED ACROSS PERSONAL ACCOUNTS" "AND PASTED PROMPTS."
Installed: the heap stirs, and the blocks rise and lock into the lattice row by row. On three walls at once, CODE, VIDEO and IMAGE write themselves from the bottom up. The camera looks down each lamp's line in turn and the blocks line up into each word. "THE RIGHT TOOLS FOR THE WORK."
Configured: the blocks slide in a wave and the lattice rebuilds: SKILLS, PROMPTS and PLAYBOOK. "SET UP FOR THE WAY YOUR TEAM WORKS."
Onboarding: three flat heads and shoulders step into the beams, one beside each word, each in its own lamp's light only. "HANDS-ON. REAL TASKS." "NOT A SLIDESHOW."
Handover: the figures step out, and a last rebuild leaves YOUR, OWN and STACK. "YOUR ACCOUNTS. YOUR KEYS." "YOURS TO KEEP."
Close: the logo placed flat, never redrawn or recoloured, on dark with "AI Stack Setup & Enablement", the URL and "A SERVICE · THIS FILM IS CODE".
Take facts only from the service page. The stack is a demo. Name no tools or models, and claim no prices and no results.
```

## How it's built

1. **Scattered.** Three lamps click on over a heap of blocks on the floor, and the walls show only noise.
2. **Installed.** The blocks rise and lock row by row, and CODE, VIDEO and IMAGE write themselves on three walls from the bottom up.
3. **Configured.** The blocks slide in a wave and the lattice rebuilds into SKILLS, PROMPTS and PLAYBOOK.
4. **Onboarding.** Three heads and shoulders step into the beams, one beside each word.
5. **Handover.** A last rebuild leaves YOUR, OWN and STACK.
6. **The mark.** The logo, the service and the link, flat on dark.

## Gotchas worth knowing

- Because the lamps are level, each row of the lattice casts the same row of every word. Build the lattice from the floor up and every word writes itself from the bottom at once, with no extra work.
- A short word can leave stray pixels its partners can't cast. Narrowing every word a little, the same for all three sets, carved all three exactly.
- If the camera stands on the middle lamp's line at the height of the lattice, the lattice hides the middle word. Stand high enough that the lattice drops below the words.
- Blocks that leave and land at random times turn the build into a blur. Let each row leave raggedly but lock together on the beat, so every row reads, and sounds, as one step.
- A figure standing in two beams casts two shadows. Narrow the beams and place each figure where only its own lamp reaches.
- The portrait cut can't be a crop: keep each shot's target, open the lens, and back the camera off along its own line until the three walls fit across.
