# R26 · The Gate · concept

**Style (reusable):** 2D vector motion design, the first of three films in this style. SVG and GSAP only, with no
canvas, no WebGL and no 3D:
- shape morphs, so one form becomes the next without a cut;
- lines drawing on along their own length;
- mattes, with shapes revealed through clip paths;
- kinetic type, set, struck out and rewritten on the beat;
- flat colour, in the product's own navy, cream and gold.

**Made for:** OutreachIQ (outreachiq.joaoqueiros.com), a self-hosted outreach workspace, MIT on GitHub. Its own line
is "The system drafts and checks. You decide what gets sent."

**The idea:** most outreach is a flood. OutreachIQ is a gate. The pipeline becomes a machine of flat shapes, and
nothing gets through until a person says yes.

1. **The flood.** One envelope multiplies into a spiral storm. A gold kill-switch line slices the frame; every
   envelope freezes, drains of colour and falls.
2. **Find.** The gold line keeps going and draws a street map. Pins drop on the beat and morph into shopfronts.
3. **Score.** Cards pass a gold dial, the needle sweeps for each, and they sort into HOT, WARM, COLD and SKIP. The
   skip lane folds away.
4. **Draft and check.** One hot card splits into three drafts, A, B and C. A scan line runs as the tone audit;
   flagged phrases are struck through and rewritten, plainer and shorter.
5. **The gate.** A monumental art-deco gate draws itself in gold hairlines. Four locks light up: duplicate contact,
   do-not-contact, tone, recipient. A dead stop, then a cursor clicks Approve; the panels fold open and one envelope
   steps through on a metronome, under a daily cap, with the kill switch in view.
6. **Title.** A reply arrow draws back, the frame collapses into the IQ mark, then the line and the URL.

**Brand rules kept in motion:**
- The IQ mark is never redrawn or recoloured. It arrives as the official tile, flat.
- The cream app colour stays on cards and envelopes, never the background. Red and amber appear only on chips and
  flags. No drop shadows, no bounce on the mark.

**Honesty:** every shopfront, name and email address is generic sample data. The lock names and the A, B and C
variants are the product's own. No result, reply rate or lead count is claimed; the reply at the end is the
product's reply detection, not a result.
