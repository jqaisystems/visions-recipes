## Intent

R02 of the Recipes library on visions.joaoqueiros.com (`/recipes`, also `/code`). A film for JQ AI Skills,
João's public library of 47 MIT skill folders (github.com/jqaisystems/jqai-ai-skills). A paper terminal
retypes the same long prompt for the 103rd time; then the sheet folds itself, step by step, into the
brand-voice-linter skill, files itself on a shelf, and the camera pulls back to all 47 skills. Concept approved
by João 2026-10-21 (`CONCEPT.md`); the folded skill is brand-voice-linter (it pairs with R01 Token Rain).
