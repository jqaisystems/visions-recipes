# R39 · Echo · concept

**Style (season 6, film 1): ink feedback.** No footage, no app screens and no slit-scan. The film is one WebGL2
feedback buffer, so the page remembers: each frame, the last page is flowed, faded towards paper and the new marks
are laid over it. Everything that moves leaves its own history behind, as ink and never as glow. The flows are a
recession towards a vanishing point, a drift, a swirl and a pull into one column. The page restarts from paper at
three anchors (open, rehearse, duet), so any frame is rebuilt the same way from its anchor. Crisp kinetic type in
Fraunces sits on a 2D layer above the ink.

**Made for:** Klatea (ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/klatea), João's private, on-device preparation studio and live
meeting copilot. Its line is "Every big conversation, rehearsed before it happens."

**The idea:** a conversation leaves a trace, and the hard moment of a call should happen twice, first in rehearsal,
then for real. On a page that remembers, the rehearsed question's ripple is still on the paper when the live
objection lands, and where the two ripples cross they make a moiré.

1. **A voice.** A pen draws a waveform and the page recedes, so the voice becomes ridgelines. "Every big conversation,"
2. **The brief.** Eight words slam in on the beat with speed lines, hold, and fly into a numbered list.
   "researched before the call,"
3. **Rehearse.** The client's question, word by word; on the impact a sage ripple fills the page. "rehearsed out loud,"
4. **Twice.** The client's objection live, word by word, with the LIVE dot; its ink ripple meets the rehearsed one in
   a moiré, then the page settles. "so the hard moment happens twice."
5. **Both sides.** Two pens, one per speaker, each as heavy as its own voice, wind into spirals inside a talk-time
   ring; a line is translated on the machine. "Both sides, transcribed on your computer."
6. **Debrief.** The page is pulled into one column, the score lands as ink and feathers out, the debrief's lines are
   wiped in. "Then an honest debrief,"
7. **The next one.** The ridgelines again. "and the prep for the next one."
8. **Close.** The mark, flat, with the line, the URL and "Free Windows beta".

**Look:** paper #F4F1EA, ink #114353, sage #6B8A67, coral #D64550 only on the LIVE dot and the closing line;
Fraunces with a sage italic accent word, IBM Plex Mono labels and a rotated margin rail; no glow, gradients or
shadows.

**Sound:** a score composed in code at 112 bpm: a pen tick per ridge, kick, brush and hat over D minor, B flat, F and
C, a slam and a pluck per word, one deep impact for the question and the same impact doubled with its echo for the
objection, two voice-like synths for the two sides (never speech) and a resolving chord.

**Honesty:** the words and figures (8/10, 44% talk time, the two-week pilot) come from the app's demo scenario, not
results, and no real meeting appears.
