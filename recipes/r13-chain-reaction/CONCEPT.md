# R13 · Chain Reaction · concept

**Style (reusable):** Chain Reaction. A marble run built from the parts of a workflow, filmed like a product
commercial. One command drops a ball, and a machine does a week of manual work on its own, station by station:
collect, clean, sort, review, send. The physics is real and baked once, so the film can bend time: slow motion
through one station, bullet time at the climax. Then the whole run pulls back into one glowing line that becomes a
word. Anyone can swap the stations for their own process: an order, an onboarding, a release.

**Made for:** AI Automation Systems, a service of JQ AI SYSTEMS. Automation is easiest to understand as a picture:
one trigger, and the repetitive work runs itself, with a person still signing it off at the review gate.

**The film:** new for the library, /code film 031. No footage, no images and no client material; the picture, the
physics and the score are all code, so the whole film ships here.
