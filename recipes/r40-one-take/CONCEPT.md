# R40 · One Take · concept

**Style: one unbroken camera.** One Canvas 2D stage, with no ink, shader or paper. The worlds are drawn in a shared
camera (a log-scale zoom with rotation), so any world can sit inside another or on the wall. Each frame is stateless
and accumulated from 16 sub-frames for real motion blur. Squash and stretch, overshoot, trim paths, shape masks, card
flips and full-frame kinetic type in Archivo.

**Made for:** Client Portal Automation (ai.joaoqueiros.com/systems/client-portal), a system João is still building.
Only its onboarding half runs today, as a local prototype; the Friday updates are planned.

**The idea:** status lives in twelve places, so the film flies through twelve worlds. The camera dives through a shape
in one to arrive in the next. At the drop it pulls back and all twelve were tiles on one wall, still playing. The wall
folds into one record.

1. **The dot.** On black, one dot falls on the kick, squashes, bounces and bursts into twelve.
2. **Twelve worlds.** Email to uploads, three beats each down to one, with no cuts.
3. **The wall.** One beat of silence, a sub drop, and the camera rockets back. "All twelve. All at once."
4. **One record.** The tiles fall in turn into one sheet. "One record per project."
5. **New project.** One button deals five cards, labelled "working locally". "One trigger. Five documents."
6. **Friday.** The days riffle, FRIDAY slams, and three client panels slide up, labelled "in development".
7. **Close.** Back to the dot, one bounce, and the mark, flat, with the line, the URL and "In development".

"One take" applies to the twelve worlds and the pull-back: later sections use wipes and one hard cut into the days.

**Look:** teal #00A89D and blue #0099D6 on ink #111110 and bone #E9E4D6, with navy, aqua, gold and coral for the colour
changes; Archivo at its heaviest, JetBrains Mono labels; no gradients or glow.

**Sound:** composed in code at 128 bpm: kick, clap and bass, a sound per world, a whoosh per dive, a riser, one beat of
silence, a sub drop on the pull-back, card flicks, a slam on FRIDAY and a chord on the logo.

**Honesty:** in development and labelled so. No results are claimed, all client text is fictional demo text, and the
labels are generic, with no product names.
