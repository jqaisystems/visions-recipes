# R27 · Ten Seconds · concept

**Style (reusable):** 2D vector motion design, the second of three films in this style. SVG and GSAP only, with no
canvas, no WebGL and no 3D:
- sound-driven geometry, with shapes built from a real loudness envelope;
- variable-width ribbons, a voice drawn as a filled stroke whose width is its loudness;
- glyphs as paths, the words baked to outlines so letters can melt into a waveform;
- branching, one ribbon splitting into many, each finding its own target;
- flat colour, in the product's own dark ember palette.

**Made for:** Tembr (ai.joaoqueiros.com/tembr), a local voice cloning studio, MIT on GitHub. Its own line is "Your
voice, everywhere you can't be."

**The idea:** a voice is a line. Tembr listens to ninety seconds, keeps the best ten, and from those ten seconds your
voice reaches every lead by name, without ever leaving your machine.

1. **Ninety seconds.** A single amber hairline breathes, then swells into a ribbon racing left to right while a timer
   runs to 01:30.
2. **Keep ten.** The camera pulls back until the whole take is one strip. A window weighs it and locks on the strongest
   ten seconds; everything outside drains and peels away.
3. **Your machine.** A frame draws on around the ribbon. The clone slides over it and locks into one. A cloud drifts in,
   never reaches it, and erases itself. 0 CLOUD CALLS · 0 API KEYS.
4. **It speaks.** A line types itself and the clone speaks it; each word's letters melt into the ribbon, shaped by the
   real loudness of that word.
5. **Everywhere.** "Hi {name}" flicks through sample names. The voice flows into one lead's page, the camera pulls out
   to a wall of them, and the ribbon branches to every page. A scan flags LONG PAUSE, REPEATED WORD and MANGLED ENDING,
   and each redraws clean.
6. **Title.** Every ribbon pulls back into one bar, then the official lockup, flat, the line and the URL.

**Brand rules kept in motion:**
- The real logo file, unchanged and flat.
- Ember only on sound that is playing; amber at rest.
- No people, padlocks or microphone illustrations. No invented numbers: only 90 s, 10 s, 0 cloud calls and 0 API keys.

**Honesty:** every name and business is generic sample data. The flag names are the product's own quality checks. No
result, reply rate or lead count is claimed, and no count of pages appears on screen.
