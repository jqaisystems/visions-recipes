# R12 · Denoise · concept

**Style (reusable):** Denoise. A prompt types out and an image resolves out of noise the way a generation
preview does: mosaic, blur, grain and grey fall away in forty visible steps while a HUD counts them. The camera
dives through the image into a page that renders card by card, each card denoising on the beat, then pulls back
into a wall of every image. Any set of images works: a portfolio, a product range, a lookbook.

**The film:** the intro for the Stills page on Imagined Worlds, /code film 013, and the first film of the site's
page intro series ("the prompt renders the page"). It has no footage and no logo, and the score is composed in
code, so the whole film ships here.

**The images:** my own Midjourney stills from the Stills page. They're included so the film runs, and they are
not licensed: swap in your own before you publish a remix.
