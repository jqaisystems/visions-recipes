# R19 · Phoropter · concept

**Style (reusable):** Phoropter. An optician's phoropter, the brass and black mask of dials and lenses, in a dark
studio, filmed in macro like a watch commercial. One problem sits on the eye chart at the far wall, out of focus;
each lens that clicks in front of it is one option, and "one, or two?" is the whole method. Anyone can relabel the
chart and the lenses for their own comparison.

**Made for:** AI Problem-to-Solution Assessment, a service of JQ AI SYSTEMS: bring one repeated workflow that wastes
time, map how it runs now, research up to three paths (an off-the-shelf tool, a connected or custom workflow, a
simpler process change), check each against pricing, integrations, privacy and limitations, and receive one
recommendation, a 30-day test plan and a decision brief within five business days.

**The film:** new for the library, /code film 037. No footage, no images and no third-party names; the workflow, the
options and the brief are made up. The picture and the score are all code, so the whole film ships here.

1. **One problem.** The chart sits across a dark room, its lines one made-up workflow ("Quotes take three days",
   "Re-typed into two systems", "Priced by hand, chased by email"), soft and unreadable.
2. **Discovery.** The phoropter swings in on its arm, the wheel turns, and the chart sharpens row by row into a
   process map: five steps, with the one that holds everything up ringed in teal.
3. **One, or two?** Three lenses click into the aperture in turn, each a different path. Through each, the chart
   redraws a different way, and five gauges on the bridge read cost, setup, risk, upkeep and undo.
4. **Checked.** The lenses line up in front of the chart and each one's checks tick in, row by row. One lens fogs and
   swings out, then another slides away.
5. **One recommendation.** The last lens comes home and locks with a click, and through the eyepiece the chart snaps
   into focus: the workflow rewritten, "Quotes out the same day" as the success metric to test, and a 30-day test
   ticking on along the bottom.
6. **The brief.** The chart turns over in flight and lands on the desk as the decision brief, stamped "Day 5", and
   light from the lens rings draws the word "clear".

**Why it travels:** everyone has sat behind a phoropter and been asked "one, or two?". It turns a research service into
a familiar ritual: one thing at a time, compared side by side, until the answer is sharp. A lens for "a simpler
process change" shows the honest part of the service, that the best answer is not always new software.

**Rules for this film:** no tool, vendor or model names on any lens or gauge. No promised savings, hours or ROI; the
success metric is shown as something to test, not a result.

**What the open source leaves out:** only the JQ AI SYSTEMS name and the service's name, line and URL.
