# R15 · Switchboard · concept

**Style (reusable):** Switchboard. A 1940s telephone switchboard rebuilt as a luxury object: smoked walnut, brass
jacks, cloth-covered patch cables with brass plugs, warm indicator lamps. Requests arrive as lamps; cables patch
themselves through the air on hanging curves; messages travel down the cables as light. Anyone can relabel the jacks
for their own tools and teams.

**Made for:** AI Workflow Integration, a service of JQ AI SYSTEMS: an AI decision layer that connects the tools a team
already uses. It routes inbound requests to the right person with a drafted reply attached, turns form entries into
enriched records, and watches a mailbox to file, summarise and escalate.

**The film:** new for the library, /code film 033. No footage, no images and no third-party logos; the jack labels are
generic on purpose. The picture and the score are all code, so the whole film ships here.

1. **Monday, 09:00.** Macro in the dark: one amber lamp blinks above INBOX, with an old telephone ring. The camera
   pulls out along the board.
2. **Route.** A cable lifts off the shelf, arcs through the air and plugs INBOX into SUPPORT with a brass click. Light
   runs down the cable and a card slides out: "Drafted reply attached".
3. **Enrich.** More lamps: FORMS into CRM, CHAT into SALES, CALENDAR into OPS, MAIL into BILLING, each on the beat
   with its own card.
4. **Decide.** The camera rises to the AI decision layer at the top of the board. One urgent lamp turns red and is
   patched straight to ON CALL: "Escalated to a person".
5. **Connect.** Six more patches in a rush until the board is a woven web, then every lamp turns green.
6. **The reveal.** The board steps back into the dark and a neon sign lights "connected", then the end card.

**Why it travels:** switchboards are iconic and tactile, the patch is a satisfying action, and cables landing on the
beat are made for short video. It explains integration in one picture: your tools stay, the connections get smart.

**What the open source leaves out:** only the JQ AI SYSTEMS name and the service's name, line and URL.
