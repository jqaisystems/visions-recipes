# R23 · Scrollwork · concept

**Style (reusable):** Cel-shaded anime. 3D rendered like a hand-drawn anime cut: flat colour in three hard bands of
light, ink outlines, screentone dots in the shadows, a painted backdrop, the drawings changing 12 times a second while
the camera moves on every frame, smear frames, impact frames and speed lines. The first of three films in this style.

**Made for:** Scrollwork (ai.joaoqueiros.com/scrollwork), the web design studio of João Queirós. Its mark is an S
drawn as a double spiral wound on a rod: a scroll on its spindle, and a scroll bar with its thumb. The page's line:
"Websites you play by scrolling."

1. **The rod.** Ink black, a dry brush stroke paints down the frame, the rod falls along it and slams.
2. **The mark.** The spiral unfurls out of the rod and spins on it over a sunburst, into a pose.
3. **The scroll.** A paper scroll unrolls through a painted night sky with the studio's six featured sites on it.
4. **The thumb.** The mark rides down a rod as the scroll thumb while the page beside it scrolls with it.
5. **The cabinet.** A manga page: seven more sites slam in as panels on the half beat.
6. **Title.** The mark settles on its rod, then the flat lockup wipes on.

**Brand rules kept in motion:** the logo is never rotated (only the spiral turns about its own rod) and the end
lockup is the flat logo, with no shading.

**What the open source leaves out:** three of the thirteen site captures (a client's site, a page of client logos and
a page of third-party posters) are replaced by plain placeholder plates under the same filenames, and the Thumbnail
Studio capture is cut off below its headline, so no thumbnail artwork ships.
Everything else ships, and the captures and the logo are not licensed.
