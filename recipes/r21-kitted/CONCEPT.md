# R21 · Kitted · concept

**Style (reusable):** Kitted. A precision tool kit in a dark workshop, filmed in macro like a product launch: a messy
pile of loose tools is sorted, a laser cuts a two-tone foam insert (a shadow board) to the exact outline of each tool
that earns a place, and every tool clicks into its own cutout with its name engraved. The kit is then opened at a row
of benches, one per person, and each bench runs a real job. Anyone can redraw the tools and the labels for their own
kit.

**Made for:** AI Stack Setup & Enablement (ai.joaoqueiros.com/services/ai-stack-setup), a service of JQ AI SYSTEMS:
an audit of what the team actually does, the right tools chosen and installed (no over-buying), custom skills and a
prompt library, image, video, webpage, report and automation workflows set up to the team's formats, a hands-on
onboarding session on real tasks, and a handover with a usage playbook.

**The film:** new for the library, /code film 039. No footage, no images and no third-party names; the team, the tools
and the jobs are made up and drawn as generic symbols. The picture and the score are all code, so the whole film
ships here.

1. **Scattered.** A work light flickers on over a steel bench: a heap of loose tools with edges in clashing colours,
   duplicates, a ring of mismatched keys, sticky notes ("pasted prompt", "v3 final") and tangled cables. The HUD:
   "everyone's using AI differently".
2. **The audit.** A teal light sheet crosses the bench. Each tool it passes is tagged with the work it does for this
   team ("Writing", "Code", "Images", "Automations", "Video", "Reports", "Pages") and marked KEEP; duplicates and
   clutter are tagged and slide off the bench into a bin: "no over-buying". The seven kept tools rise and hover.
3. **Cut to fit.** A case slides in and its lid opens on blank charcoal foam. A laser head comes down and cuts one
   outline per tool, the first in macro, the rest at speed, with sparks and smoke; each plug pops out to show the teal
   layer underneath. The last outline is an empty slot.
4. **Installed and configured.** On the beat, each tool drops into its cutout, its edge turns teal and its engraved
   name lights. Twenty prompt cards riffle into a box beside the case.
5. **Onboarding.** Four more benches light up; the same kit slides in and opens at each one in sync, and over every
   bench a job runs in light: a weekly report, a product shot, a landing page, a social cut, an invoice check.
6. **Handed over.** A playbook lands in the empty slot and the laser engraves the word "equipped" into the foam of
   the lid. End on "AI Stack Setup & Enablement", "Your AI stack, installed and running properly." and the URL.

**Why it travels:** a perfectly kitted foam case is one of the most satisfying images there is. It turns a setup
engagement into one picture anyone gets: from a messy pile to a kit where every tool has its place, then the same kit
at every desk.

**Rules for this film:** no tool, vendor or model names anywhere, and no logos; the tools are symbols (a nib, a
spanner, a lens, a gear, a film gate, a report sheet, a page frame). No promised savings, hours or ROI. The team and
the sticky-note names are made up.

**What the open source leaves out:** only the JQ AI SYSTEMS name and the service's name, line and URL.
